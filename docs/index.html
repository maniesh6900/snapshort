SnapDrive

One shared camera roll for your team, straight into Google Drive.

SnapDrive is an Android camera app for small teams. Photos and videos you capture are uploaded to a single shared Google Drive folder, with a subfolder for each person.

Android app · Google Drive storage · No ads, no tracking

What the app does

  • Take photos and record videos with a simple camera screen (zoom, front/rear camera, flash/torch, grid).
  • Upload each capture automatically to your team's shared Google Drive folder, inside a subfolder named after the person who captured it.
  • Join a team with a name and a shared team passcode. No SnapDrive account, email address, or password is stored by the app.
  • Keep a queue of uploads with clear pending, in-progress, failed, and completed states, plus retry for failures.

Google Drive access

SnapDrive's backend is connected to Google Drive by the team's administrator using Google's OAuth consent flow. The app only creates files and folders inside the one Drive folder the administrator configures; it does not read, modify, or delete your other Drive files. Uploaded media belongs to the Google account that owns that folder.

Privacy

SnapDrive does not sell data, show ads, or use analytics. It does not collect your contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.

Read the full SnapDrive Privacy Policy for details on what is stored and how to delete it.
